ChitarraTune is a guitar tuner for iPhone, iPad and Mac that does one thing and does it well: it tells you, quickly and steadily, how far each string is from where it should be.

PRECISE
• Pitch detection measures the fundamental of the string itself, so the sharp overtones of real steel strings don't pull the reading off.
• A smooth needle, the note, the frequency and the deviation in cents.
• "In tune" lights up only once the note has really settled inside the ±5 cent zone.

TEN TUNINGS
Standard, Half step down, Full step down, Drop D, Drop C, DADGAD, Open D, Open G, Open E and Open A. The tuner recognizes which string you play, or you can pin one string and tune just that.

YOUR PITCH
Set A4 anywhere from 415 to 466 Hz: baroque pitch, 432 Hz, or the out-of-tune piano at a friend's house.

YOUR WAY
• Note names in English (C D E F G A B) or fixed-do solfège (Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si).
• Dial or bar gauge.
• On the Mac, choose any microphone or audio interface and open one tuner per window.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Sound is analyzed on your device as you play and thrown away a fraction of a second later. ChitarraTune never records audio, has no network access at all, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

CONSIDERATE
• Stops listening after a period of silence, and slows its analysis in Low Power Mode.
• VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and full keyboard control.
• Siri and Shortcuts: start and stop tuning, pick a tuning, a string, an input or a reference pitch.

ChitarraTune is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. It is open source under the BSD license.
