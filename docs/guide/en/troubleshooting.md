# If ChitarraTune doesn't hear your guitar
<!-- description: What to do when the needle doesn't move, shows the wrong string or listening stops. -->

## The needle doesn't move

- Make sure ChitarraTune may use the microphone: choose Apple menu > System Settings, click **Privacy & Security**, click **Microphone**, then turn on ChitarraTune.
- Check the thin level bar under the gauge. If it doesn't move while you play, ChitarraTune hears nothing: choose another input from the **Input** menu. See [Choose a microphone or audio interface](input.md).
- Play a little closer to the microphone. ChitarraTune adapts to how quiet your room is, but it cannot hear a string that is quieter than the room itself.

## It shows the wrong string

Click the string you are tuning, below the gauge: ChitarraTune then listens only for that string. Click **Auto** to go back.

## A message replaces the tuner

| Message | What to do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Microphone Access Is Off** | Click **Open Settings** and turn on ChitarraTune in Microphone, then click **Try Again**. |
| **Microphone Restricted** | Access is limited by Screen Time or a device profile; ask whoever manages the device. |
| **No Audio Input** | Connect a microphone or an audio interface, then click **Try Again**. |
| **Input Unavailable** | Choose another input, then try again. |
| **Couldn't Start Listening** | Quit any other app that uses the microphone, then click **Try Again**. |
| **Listening Interrupted** | A call or another app took the microphone. Listening resumes by itself when it ends. |

## Listening stopped by itself

After a few minutes without sound ChitarraTune stops listening, so the microphone doesn't drain the battery. To change how long it waits, or to turn this off, see [Change ChitarraTune settings](settings.md).

## Send a report

If the problem stays, choose ChitarraTune > About ChitarraTune, click **Copy Diagnostics** and paste the text into an email to the developer or into a report on the project's page (choose Help > Report an Issue). The report holds the app and system versions and the app's own recent log: never sound, never personal data. It also says why ChitarraTune set sound aside, for example because it was too quiet for the room.

**On iPhone or iPad:** **Copy Diagnostics** is in Settings, under **About**.
