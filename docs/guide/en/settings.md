# Change ChitarraTune settings
<!-- description: Reference pitch, note names, gauge, haptics and when to stop listening. -->

Choose ChitarraTune > Settings, or press Command-Comma.

| Setting | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Reference Pitch (A4)** | The pitch of the A above middle C, from 415 to 466 Hz. **Reset to 440 Hz** returns to the standard. See [Set the reference pitch](reference-pitch.md). |
| **Note Names** | **C D E F G A B**, **Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si**, or **Automatic**, which follows the language of your device: solfège in Italian, Spanish, French, Portuguese and other languages that use it. |
| **Gauge** | **Dial**, a needle on an arc, or **Bar**, a horizontal scale. |
| **Haptics** | A soft tap when a string reaches pitch, on devices that support it. |
| **Stop When Silent** | Stops listening after 1, 3 or 5 minutes without sound, or **Never**. |

Settings are kept on your device and apply to every tuner window.

**On iPhone or iPad:** Tap the Settings button at the top of the screen, then tap **Done**.
