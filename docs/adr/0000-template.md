# ADR 0000: Template

Status: Template

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraint, what goes wrong today. Written so that someone who was not there can judge the decision on its own.

## Decision

1. Numbered rules, each with MUST or MUST NOT. One rule, one sentence where possible.
2. A rule a machine cannot check says who checks it and when.
3. Whatever the rule forbids, say what to do instead.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it rules out, and what becomes easier. Honest about the friction it adds.

## Enforcement

- The path of at least one test, script or workflow that fails when a rule is broken. An ADR without enforcement is an intention, not a decision ([ADR 0001](0001-record-architecture-decisions.md) rule 4).
