Set the reference pitch

Most music is tuned so that the A above middle C, A4, is 440 Hz. To play with an instrument or an ensemble tuned differently, change the reference pitch.

  1. Choose ChitarraTune > Settings, or press Command-Comma.

  2. In Calibration, drag the Reference Pitch (A4) slider, from 415 to 466 Hz.

To return to the standard pitch, click Reset to 440 Hz.

The reference pitch applies to every tuning and every window, and stays set until you change it. 415 Hz is common for baroque music; some musicians prefer 432 Hz.

On iPhone or iPad: Tap the Settings button at the top of the screen.
