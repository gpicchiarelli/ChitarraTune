Keyboard shortcuts

ActionShortcut
Start or stop listeningCommand-L
Detect the string automaticallyCommand-0
Tune string 1 to 6Command-1 to Command-6
New tuner windowCommand-N
SettingsCommand-Comma
Close the windowCommand-W
Quit ChitarraTuneCommand-Q

With Full Keyboard Access turned on in System Settings > Accessibility > Keyboard, press Tab to move between controls and Space to press the selected one.

On iPad with a keyboard: The same shortcuts work. Hold down Command to see them.
