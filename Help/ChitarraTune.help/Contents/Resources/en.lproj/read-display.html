Read the tuner

While ChitarraTune listens, the window shows:

ItemWhat it tells you
NoteThe string ChitarraTune hears, with its octave, for example E2.
HzThe frequency it measures, in hertz.
CentsHow far the string is from its pitch. A semitone is 100 cents; −12 means 12 cents too low.
Needle or barThe same distance, drawn on a scale from −50 to +50 cents. The green zone is ±5 cents.
StatusFlat (too low), Sharp (too high), Almost there (within 15 cents) or In tune.
LevelThe thin bar under the gauge shows that the microphone hears you.

In tune appears only once the string has stayed within 5 cents of its pitch for a moment, and stays until the string drifts more than 7 cents away, so the display does not flicker while you adjust the peg.

When the string stops ringing, the last reading stays on screen, dimmed, for a moment, so you can still read it.

Tip: Colors are never the only signal: each state also has its own word and symbol.

To change between the dial and the bar, or the way note names are written, see Change ChitarraTune settings.
