Tune your guitar

  1. Click Start Listening at the bottom of the window.

    The first time, ChitarraTune explains why it needs the microphone. Click Continue, then allow access when your Mac asks.

  2. Play one open string and let it ring.

    ChitarraTune shows the name of the string it hears, the frequency in hertz and how many cents the string is from its pitch.

  3. Turn the tuning peg slowly:

    • If the display says Flat and suggests Tighten the string, raise the pitch.
    • If it says Sharp and suggests Loosen the string, lower the pitch.

  4. When the needle stays in the green zone, the display turns green and says In tune. Move on to the next string.

  5. When you finish, click Stop.

ChitarraTune also stops by itself after a few minutes without sound, so it does not use the microphone if you forget. See Change ChitarraTune settings.

Tip: Press Command-L to start or stop listening.

On iPhone or iPad: Tap Start Listening, then tap Stop when you finish. ChitarraTune stops listening when you leave the app.
