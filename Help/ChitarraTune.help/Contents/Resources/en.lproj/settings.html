Change ChitarraTune settings

Choose ChitarraTune > Settings, or press Command-Comma.

SettingWhat it does
Reference Pitch (A4)The pitch of the A above middle C, from 415 to 466 Hz. Reset to 440 Hz returns to the standard. See Set the reference pitch.
Note NamesC D E F G A B, Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si, or Automatic, which follows the language of your device: solfège in Italian, Spanish, French, Portuguese and other languages that use it.
GaugeDial, a needle on an arc, or Bar, a horizontal scale.
HapticsA soft tap when a string reaches pitch, on devices that support it.
Stop When SilentStops listening after 1, 3 or 5 minutes without sound, or Never.

Settings are kept on your device and apply to every tuner window.

On iPhone or iPad: Tap the Settings button at the top of the screen, then tap Done.
