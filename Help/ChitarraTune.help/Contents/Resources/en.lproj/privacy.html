Privacy and the microphone

ChitarraTune uses the microphone only while it is listening, to measure the pitch of your instrument.

  • Sound stays on your device. It is analyzed as you play and discarded a fraction of a second later. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

  • No network. ChitarraTune cannot connect to the internet, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

  • Your settings stay on your device, and are removed when you delete the app.

  • Diagnostics leave your device only if you copy them and send them yourself. See If ChitarraTune doesn't hear your guitar.

When ChitarraTune listens, your Mac shows that the microphone is in use in the menu bar. Click Stop to release it; it is released too when you close the window or quit.

The full privacy policy is available from the project's page.
