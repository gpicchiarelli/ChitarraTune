Use more than one tuner

On your Mac you can tune more than one instrument at once, or keep one window for each input.

  • Open a new tuner: Choose File > New Tuner Window, or press Command-N.

Each window has its own input, tuning and chosen string. The reference pitch and the other settings are shared.

Siri, Shortcuts, the Dock menu and the Start Tuning control act on the window you used last. Closing a window stops its tuner.

Tip: Control-click the ChitarraTune icon in the Dock and choose Start Listening or Stop Listening to control the tuner without switching to it.
