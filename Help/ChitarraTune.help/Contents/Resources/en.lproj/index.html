ChitarraTune User Guide

ChitarraTune listens to your guitar and shows how far each string is from its pitch, quickly and steadily, so you can tune by ear and by eye at the same time.

Tune in seconds

Choose a tuning, play an open string and turn the tuning peg until the display turns green. ChitarraTune recognizes the string you play, or listens only for the one you choose.

Tune your guitar

Ten tunings, your pitch

From Standard to Open A, and with the A above middle C anywhere from 415 to 466 Hz, for baroque pitch, 432 Hz or a piano that has drifted.

Change the tuning · Set the reference pitch

Private by design

ChitarraTune measures sound on your device and throws it away a fraction of a second later. It never records, has no network access and collects no data.
