Choose a microphone or audio interface

ChitarraTune uses your Mac's current input unless you choose another.

  1. Click the Input menu in the toolbar.

  2. Choose a microphone or an audio interface, or System Default to follow the input chosen in System Settings.

You can plug in or unplug a device while ChitarraTune is open: the menu updates by itself, and if the device you chose disappears, ChitarraTune goes back to the system default.

With an audio interface that has several inputs, ChitarraTune listens to the one your guitar is plugged into, whichever channel that is.

On iPhone or iPad: Connect the microphone or interface before you start listening. The input menu at the top of the screen lists the inputs the device offers once listening has started.
